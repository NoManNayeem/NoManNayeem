## <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/feather-icons@4.29.2/dist/icons/compass.svg" width="18" height="18" alt="icon"/> Idea → Research → Development Loop

<p align="center">
  <img src="./idea-research-development-loop.svg" alt="Animated Idea Research Development loop" width="720"/>
</p>

- Idea: capture the signal, constraints, and success criteria
- Research: validate hypotheses, data readiness, and evaluation strategy
- Data & Prototype: build the experiment harness and iterate quickly
- Development: productionize end-to-end AI/agent workflows and automation
- Evaluate: run robust offline + online checks with monitoring and governance
- Deploy: ship with CI/CD, rollout control, and reliability focus
- Feedback: measure outcomes and refine models, prompts, and policies
- Scale: expand across teams, products, and multi-country operations
